You start at LVL 1. You must gain 100XP in order to proceed to the next level
## Combat Style
At LVL 1 You Choose one of the _Apprentice Technique_ : [[Bastion]], [[Sage]], [[Striker]] [[Warden]]. You gain all maneuvers of the _Apprentice Techniques_ when acquired 

At LVL 2,3,5 and 6 You Choose Styles: [[Blade Style|Blade]], [[Dark Magic Style|Dark Magic]] , [[Shadow Style|Shadow]], [[Tactician Style|Tactician]], [[White Magic Style|White Magic]] . 
You spend "XP points" which can be used to unlock a new maneuver or level up an old one. When you choose a new style you gain 2 "XP points" The second time you choose it you gain 1 "XP point". You can't have more than 2 Styles.

At LVL 4 and 7 you gain an Specialization
## Life Paths
You start choosing a Path at LVL 1, each level you add +2 to a Verb of your choosing at LVL 5 you may choose a Second Path
## Profession 
You increase your Professions and Hobbies by +1 every 2 levels starting at level 2
