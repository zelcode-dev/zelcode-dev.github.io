### What is a TTRPG?
Tabletop Role-Playing Games (TTRPGs) are collaborative storytelling adventures where players take on the roles of characters in a shared imaginary world. One player takes on the role of the **Game Master (GM)**, who guides the story, describes the world, and controls the challenges the players face. The other players create and control their own characters, making decisions and rolling dice to determine the outcomes of their actions.
