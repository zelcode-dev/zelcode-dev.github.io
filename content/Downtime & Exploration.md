#### Time Frames
Days are separated into 6 Time Frames ***Mañana, Tarde, Noche, Madrugada Temprana, Madrugada Tardía*** , each lasting 4 hours, each one is called a **Time Frame**
#### Wilderness and Survival
A character must feed themselves and drink enough water to get through each day, one Sustenance "Item" counts as enough food and water for the day, be it 1u of a Ration or buying some stew at the tavern.
A character must sleep a total of **2 Time Frames** per day or be [[Fatigued]], Guard rotations for the Night do not count against the these **2 Time Frames** but must be equally divided amongst all able party members.
Characters usually need a source of light to see in the darkness, having to hold a source of light in the hand gives [[Cumbersome]]
#### Resting
When your character goes to rest he recovers some **HP**, for each **Time Frame** he rests a character recovers **2d6** of HP. Resting **2 Time Frames** in a secure and comfortable place recovers full **HP**, this is called a ***Full Rest***

Players may spend a week of downtime resting and healing **[[Stress]]**, they heal **1d6** per **Week** spent resting
#### Downtime Activities
When there's more than a week of time between active adventuring, characters are open to Downtime activities, they are the things that characters have been doing between "episodes" they usually don't have major roleplaying scenes and requiere only a recap

##### Crafting
Once per Week of Downtime a character may roll their relevant [[Skill System#Professions|Profession]] , **Success** fills  one face of the clock, **Critical Success** fills two, **Failure** is static, **Critical Failure** risk the result of the crafting coming with a draw back, be the item being cursed or finding some unwanted truth

During adventures players may encounter some _Materials_ , they usually have some magical quality, when crafting players may choose what to make of the material (Armor, Ring, Bow, Sword) and the special effects that the crafting gives is related to the Material, chosen by the GM, Clock size to complete this project and required gold are determined by the end result.
Crafting despite it's name can have many meanings such as _Research_ or _Building a Fortress_ which may not always requiere _Materials_ but usually need more _Gold_
##### Bonding
Players may work their [[Bond]] during **Downtime**, if the bond requieres a higher duty (_For Example working for a Secret Cult_) they may not go up a tier during **Downtime** but each week spent represents accumulated effort, this effort may prompt NPCs to give objectives which failure or success determines if the **Bond** goes up into a **Tier** or not. **Clocks** to fill out for going up into the bond are drawn by the GM before the players chooses to spend time with the **Bond**. Each week doesn't mean the player spent 7 whole days with a character, but preparations and stress from the encounters
##### Investigation
If the players enter Downtime with the intent to prepare for an adventure they can use this time to investigate the site of their next adventure, be it a Dungeon, a Bank to Heist, A Noble to kill. Each week they may roll a relevant [[Skill System|Skill]], **Success** grants one piece of relevant information, **Critical Success** grants rare and some really helpful information. **Critical Failure** has the risk of alerting their adversaries of their plans 
#### Danger during Downtime and Twists
The factions that be also move during Downtime, for each week spent in Downtime the clocks of the other factions that are moving on the world also seek to complete their plans. The results of this movements may be visible to the players after they might be too late, but usually they will get a glimpse of them.
For each **Month** of Downtime there's a greater chance of a Twist (or multiple). GM rolls for a _Twist_, Twist are some _dramatic_ actions that happend between adventures, a Character may find clues to their brother's murder, they may hear a group of strange mercenaries are looking for them, someone stole the local bank and the guards seem to be extra careful
