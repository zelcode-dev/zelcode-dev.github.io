Each character has an **Armor** slot and **4 Equipment** slots, and a carrying capacity equal to 7 + **Body**. Equipped items count towards the total carrying capacity 
 Some items come in bundles(quiver of arrows) this items are considered always at their max weight  until they are empty. **Light**  (L)Items occupy 1/2 a slot 

Some items may be too heavy for one character, if an object occupies more slots that a character has they may shares slots to carry an item with another character and both gain the [[Fatigued]] condition.

Currencies don't have weight, the format is Copper Coins (cc) Silver Coins (sc) and Gold Coins (gc)
10 copper coins are equal to 1 silver coin, and 10 silver coins are equal to 1 gold coin
