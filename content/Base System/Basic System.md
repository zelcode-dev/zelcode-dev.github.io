
## Main Attributes
Each character has 3 main attributes which represent their general strengths and weaknesses 
- **Soul** represents a character’s connection to the divine, emotions, artistic drive, that which comes from the heart, main attribute around [[Stress]]
- **Grace** represents both reflexes, sophisticated movement, that which comes from the wrists BUT also academic knowledge, the studying from the books, that which comes from the mind, helps with the [[Doom Dice]]
- **Body** represents a character’s physical strength, endurance, muscular power, that which comes from the muscles, main attribute around [[Inventory|Carrying Capacity]] and [[Combat#HP|HP]]
### Rolling the Dice
The **basic roll system** is primarily used outside of combat. When prompted for an **Easy**, **Normal**, or **Hard** check, you are being asked to make this type of roll.
>[!info]- No Unnecessary rolls
>A Roll is not asked for if failure is not interesting or success is not possible:  
Breaking a wooden door might be difficult but with all the time of the day, is not interesting, that's not a dice roll. _Now_ if you need to break a door because a hoard of goblins is chasing you, now that's a dice roll.
#### Basic Roll
Basic rolls have a target number determined by the difficulty minus your relevant **Attribute Modifier**. The  difficulties are: **Easy** (10) **Normal** (15) **Hard** (20). You roll a **d20** against the target number to determine success. 
>[!Example]- Example 
> You have a **3** on **Body** and you're attempting to to free a child with his leg crushed by a heavy rock, the GM says it would be a **Normal** roll. You would grab **15** (Normal Difficulty) subtract **3** (Your Body), leaving you with **12** for this roll, so you would roll a **d20** and any result equal or greater than **12** you succeed
##### [[Banes and Boons]]
Certain special abilities or effects may add **Banes** or **Boons** to a roll. A **Boon** is a **1d4** that is added to your roll, while a **Bane** is a **1d4** that is subtracted. Banes and Boons cancel each other out; for example, 2 Banes and 1 Boon result in **1 Bane**. 
>[!Example]- Example 
> Let's say you rolled a **10** (A failure) but your other party members where helping you, giving 1 **Boon** to the roll, you roll that Boon's D4 and it comes out a **2**, you add that to your roll with a total of **12** and that Failure became a Succes
##### Versus Rolls
For a **Versus Roll**, both participants roll a **d20** and add their relevant **Attribute Modifier**. The higher total wins the contest. In the event of a tie, you can either play out the tie narratively or, if the outcome would be uninteresting, simply re-roll as if the tie never occurred.
>[!info]- Not to be abused
>Versus rolls usually should be used for simple task, like _Who wins an archery competition_, more complex interactions should be the result of various rolls and done with the basic system, using things like [Clocks](https://bladesinthedark.com/progress-clocks)
##### CRITICAL
A **natural 20** is considered a **Critical Roll**. if there was 0% chance of success the critical roll is just a normal success and you add **1  [[Doom Dice]]**
A **Natural 1** is considered a **Critical Failure**, they add **1 [[Doom Dice]]** and they get **2d6 Stress**
