The Doom Dice are a pool of **D4** which get bigger. After you failed a roll you may opt to use the **Doom Dice** , if you do you add the amount of Dice in the pool to your roll _but_ you must roll all of the Dice in the pile.
Doom Dice can be used during Combat by adding the amount of Doom Dice to one damage roll

The Dice may come up in your favor or negative, count the faces of each die, **1s  and 2s** count as a **negative**, **3s and 4s** count as a **positive**, if you end up with a tie, **add and roll** another **D4**
- If the result is negative :  Sum **All** of the dices. The character that pushed the roll takes that amount of **[[Stress]]** damage
- If the result is positive and  you heal the sum of all dice in both *[[Stress]] and [[Combat#HP|HP]]*, and if you got **Quads** you gain a [[Belief and Sin#Hope|Hope]]
When Pushing a Roll and failing a character can reduce the amount of [[Stress]] they take equal to their [[Basic System#Main Attributes|Grace]] **X 1,5** (Rounded Up)
