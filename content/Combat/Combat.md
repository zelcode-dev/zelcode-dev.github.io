Combat always has a motivation but the baseline between them is violence to attain objectives, when officially starting combat call for a roll to determine the Turn Order. Then the game turns to Combat Mode 
#### Initiative
Each player goes in order according to their **Grace** from highest to lowest. In the case of a tie, roll a 1d6 to tie it off.
#### Battle Map
All combat is done in a 8x8 grid 
### Actions and Maneuvers
Each Unit gets **2** Actions per Turn which they can spend on Maneuvers , Maneuvers are the _stuff_ you can do in combat, they determine (and limit) what you can do during combat.
By default, all Units get all **Basic Maneuvers** which are: 
- [[Combat#Attack|Attack]]
- [[Combat#Move|Move]]
- [[Combat#Interact|Interact]]
- [[Combat#React|React]]
#### Attack
**Cost** : 1 ; **Type** : Attack
- Defined by weapon in hand
- Range is the amount of squares that the target must be from you to attack
- Roll the weapon's damage, which gets subtracted firstly from **Armor** and after from **HP**.
- All Damages have Types. By Default damage is Physical
  <font color="#953734">CRITICAL</font>
When all the dice of your damage roll maxes out, it's considered <font color="#953734">CRITICAL</font> you roll an extra unit of your damage roll, if that dice is also his possible maximum, your roll another, and so forth
>[!Example]- 
> If your damage roll is **2d4** and you roll { **8** , **8** } then you'd roll an additional  **1d4** if that dice is { **4** } your roll another **1d4** and so forth until it reaches it's maximum 
> The ending number will be your damage for that roll
> My damage is **2d6** and you roll {**6**, **6**}, so I roll another 1d6, which comes up as { 6 }, I roll another one that comes up {6 }, so AGAIN I roll another one, that one comes up as a {5}, so we sum them all up 6 + 6 + 6 + 6 + 5 = 29 which will be our final damage

#### Move
**Cost** : 1 ; **Type** : Move
- Move into a new square, you move 1 square at a time
- Cannot move diagonally
#### React
**Cost** : ?  **Type** : Utility
- Spend an amount of actions equal to another maneuver
- Set up a Condition (When an enemy walks in front, when someone touches that object)
- If the condition is met before your next turn, you interrupt the turn of the other to perform the maneuver you set up
#### Interact
**Cost** : 1 **Type** : Utility
An Interaction is a list of recurring actions witch requiere some time or difficulty and so they can't be made seamlessly.
The following list are Interact Maneuvers : 
- Taking an item out of a backpack or container
- Feeding another willing character a consumable
### HP 
HP is calculated as : 30 + **Body** x 2. When **HP** reaches 0 a character falls unconscious, going into the negative numbers a character rolls in each of his turns a **d20** -  **each point below 0 HP**.  Failure means they are **D E A D**
They will resume into normal state when they reach into positive **HP** again

 Another character may assist into keeping alive a character that's dying, if they have a proper **[[Skill System#Hobby|Hobby]]** available to them they can administer **Basic Aid** with **2 actions** this will keep the character stable (Having him not roll to see if he dies) for 1 **[[Downtime & Exploration#Time Frames|Time Frame]]**. With a proper **[[Skill System#Professions|Profession]]** they can keep the character stable for 2  **[[Downtime & Exploration#Time Frames|Time Frame]]**
#### Armor 
Armor works exactly as HP but gets fully repaired when resting 1 [[Downtime & Exploration#Time Frames|Time Frame]], all benefits from Armor are applied only when worn. When Armor reaches 0 the benefits of the Armor stop applying
