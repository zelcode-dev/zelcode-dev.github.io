
### Ramming
**Cost** : 2 ; **Type**: Attack
You sprint in a straight line, if you collide into a unit. That Unit gets [[Push]] X+1 Opposite to you. 
Increase the damage they receive from pushing into a Wall equal to X
X is equal to the amount of squares you traveled 
**LVL 2** : Add 2 to X
### Wall Maker
**Cost** : 2 ; **Type**: Utilty; **Range**: 3
You lift a Wall of Stone in any square, you may only have 1 Active Wall at any time. You can use 1 action to move a Stone Wall if there's already a Stone Wall in place
**LVL 2** :You may only have 2 Active Walls at any time
### Wrestling 
**Cost** : 2 ; **Type**: Attack; **Range**: 1
You grab an enemy and throw them into any other square. They gain [[Fractured]] till the ending of  their next turn. If a Unit is thrown into another they both receive 1d6 of Damage. The Unit that was not thrown gets [[Push]] 1 Opposite of you
**LVL 2** : Damage of Throwing into another Unit increases to 2d4
### Dive Bomb
**Cost** : 2 ; **Type**: Attack;
You throw yourself into any square, Units adjacent to your dive spot get [[Push]] 2 Opposite of You and Receive 1d6 damage. Units located at your dive spot receive 1d6+3 of damage and get [[Push]] 1 in any direction you choose.
**LVL 2** : Damage increases from 1d6 to 1d6 + 2 and from 1d6 + 3 to 2d4+2
### Grappling Hook
**Cost** : 2 ; **Type**: Attack ;**Range** : 3
Select a Unit. They get [[Push]] into a square adjacent to You. After they get pushed you may perform a melee range Attack as a part of this Manuever 
**LVL 2** Get [[Boost]] 3 on the Melee Attack
