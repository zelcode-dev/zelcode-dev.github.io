### Major Blessing
**Cost** : 2 ; **Type**: Utility  
Select a Square. All Units on that square get [[Boost]] 2. You cannot repeat this maneuver as long as there's a Blessed Spot in the ground. You can use 1 action to move the square if there's already a Blessing in place
**LVL 2** : Upgrade [[Boost]] 2 to [[Boost]] 3
### Fire Bomb
**Cost** : 1 **Type** : Attack **Range**: 2
Deal 1d6 + 2 of Fire Damage to a Unit. The Unit of the bomb gets set on [[Fire]] (Burning)
**LVL 2** : **Global Passive** Enemies need 2 Interact actions to snuff out _their own_ [[Fire]] (Burning)
### Fog Bomb
**Cost** : 1 **Type** : Attack 
Select one square, that squares gets clouded on [[Fog]]. If an enemy was already on that square you do 1d6 Ice Damage
**LVL 2** : Upgrade from 1d6 to 2d6 of Ice Damage
### Inspiring Rally
**Cost** : 1 ; **Type**: Utility  
Select a Unit. This unit gets 1 more action on their next turn
**LVL 2** : Increases the amount of actions given from 1 to 2
