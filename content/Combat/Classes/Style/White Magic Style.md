
### Major Healing
**Cost** : 1 ; **Type**: Utility  
Select one Ally they heal for 1d6+2 **HP** 
**LVL 2**: Heal increases to 1d6+4 HP
### Shield
**Cost** : 2 ; **Range**: 3 **Type**: Utility  
Select a Unit and give them [[Shield]]
**LVL 2** : **Global Passive,** A Unit that steps into an Environmental effect with [[Shield]] snuffs out that Environmental Effect
### Sacred Spot
**Cost** : 2 Actions;  **Type**: Utility  
Select a Square. All Units on that square get [[Defense]] 2. You cannot repeat this maneuver as long as there's a Sacred Spot in the ground. You can use 1 action to move the square if there's already a Spot in place
**LVL 2** : [[Defense]] goes from 2 to 4
### Holy Parry
**Cost** : 2 Actions; [[Reaction]]; **Range**: 2 ; **Type**: Utility  
When an ally takes damage, perform this ability. You roll the same dice and bonuses as the damage source, you heal an amount of damage equal to this roll + 5
**LVL 2** : You do an amount of damage to the source of the damage equal to x1/2 your healing done with _Holy Parry_.
