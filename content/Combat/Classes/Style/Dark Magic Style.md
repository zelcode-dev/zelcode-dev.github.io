###  Ember Steal
**Cost** : 1 [[Reaction]]; **Type** : Utility; 
Whenever an Attack maneuver is performed, of a damage type not physical you may reduce the damage by 1d6, convert all damage another unit deals into the Type of that attack.
You can destroy environmental effects and convert your damage.
- [[Fog]] = Ice
- [[Fire]] = Fire
This effect last till end of combat or ended at will for free
**LVL 2** : Reduce damage increases from 1d6 to 1d6+2 
### Lighting Strike
**Cost** : 2 ; **Type** : Attack
Do 1d6 Lighting Damage to all squares in a straight line. The line gets stop by any kind of Wall 
All squares also get struck by [[Lighting]]
**LVL 2** : Upgrade from 1d6 to 1d6+3 of Lighting Damage
### Fire Strike
**Cost** : 1 **Type** : Attack; **Range** : 3
Select one square, that squares gets set on [[Fire]]. If an enemy was already on that square you do 1d6 Fire Damage
**LVL 2** : Upgrade from 1d6 to 2d6 of Fire Damage
### Cold and Terrible Air
**Cost** : 1 **Type** : Utility; **Range** : 3
Select one square affected by [[Fog]]. The [[Fog]] turns into [[Ice]]. If there was already an enemy on that square they receive 1d6 +2 Ice Damage
**LVL 2** : Upgrade from 1d6+2 to 2d6+2 of Ice Damage
