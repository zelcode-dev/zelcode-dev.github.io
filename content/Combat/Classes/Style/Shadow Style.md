
### Sneaky Sneaky Sneaky
**Cost** : 2 ; **Type**: Utility
You turn yourself [[Invisible]], coming out of invisibility with an attack gives you [[Boost]] 2 for that turn
**LVL 2** : Increase [[Boost]] from 2 to 3
### Elemental Stab
**Cost** : 2 ; **Type**: Attack
As part of this maneuver, perform an attack. If the target of your attack is currently being affected by [[Fog]] or [[Fire]] you deal 2d4+1 of additional damage (Fire if Fire, Ice if Fog) after dealing the damage you snuff or clear the effect on the unit or environment
**LVL 2**: You can also perform Elemental Stab on a [[Ice|Frozen]] unit dealing 2d4+1 of Ice damage ignoring the Frozen negation
### Needle Striker
**Passive**
When performing an attack with a weapon of 1 singular die, multiply your [[Boost]] x 3
**LVL 2**: You Multiply your [[Boost]] by x4 instead of x3
### Witch's Curse
**Cost** : 1 Actions ; **Type**: Utility 
Select a Unit. That Unit gets [[Debuff]] 2 until your next turn
**LVL 2**: The [[Debuff]] increases from 2 to 3
