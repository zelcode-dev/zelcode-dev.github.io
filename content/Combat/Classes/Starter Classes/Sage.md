### Basic Healing
**Cost** : 1 action ; **Type**: Utility  
Select one Ally they heal for 1d6 **HP**
### Healing Parry
**Cost** : 2 action; [[Reaction]] ; **Type**: Utility  
When an ally takes damage, perform this ability. You roll the same dice and bonuses as the damage source, you heal an amount of damage equal to this roll.
