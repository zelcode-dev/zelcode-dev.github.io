
### Blessing
**Cost** : 1 Action; **Type**: Utility  
Select any unit, that unit gets [[Boost]] 1d4 until your next turn.
### Curse
**Cost** : 2 Actions ; **Type**: Utility  
Select a Square. Units on that square get [[Debuff]] 1
This effect lasts till end of combat. This Ability cannot be repeated as long as the _Cursed_ square persists. If there is a Cursed Square in place you may use 1 action to move it into another square
