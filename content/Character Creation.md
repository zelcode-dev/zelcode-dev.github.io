
When creating a new character, first determine your **Race** and your **[[Skill System#Culture|Culture]]** you distribute your **[[Basic System#Main Attributes|Attributes]]** as follows: one at **-2**, one at **1**, and one at **3**.
A Character's **HP** is equal to **30** + **Body** x2
A Character's **Stress** is equal to **15** + **Soul** x2
Select one **[[Skill System#Professions|Profession]]** at **+3**  2 **[[Skill System#Hobby|Hobbies]]** at **+3** and your **[[Skill System#Culture|Culture]]** starts at **+5**
Start with 2 **[[Belief and Sin#Beliefs|Belief]]** and 2 **[[Belief and Sin#Sins|Sins]]** at **4**
Choose 1 [[Paths]] you start with 1 [[Paths#Rituals|Sacred Verbs]] at +2
