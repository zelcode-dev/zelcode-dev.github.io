
A character has a limited capacity for **Stress**. Max **Stress** is equal to 15 + ( **Soul** x **2** )
When a character reaches their **Max Stress** they gain the [[Fatigued]] condition. Reaching double their **Max Stress** you must roll a **Normal Soul** check:
- Success makes a [[Belief and Sin#Sins|Sin]] worse by **2** , you burst out and reset your stress to **half it's Max.** 
- Failure gives your character a **HEART ATTACK** resulting in **Instant Death** 

You can do this roll with a **+5** by making one of your [[Bond]] go down **1 Tier**
