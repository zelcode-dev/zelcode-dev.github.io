# Rituals
You gain the ability to perform actions beyond normal human capacity.
You must choose to do a Ritual within your own Path. A Hunter can use rituals to track creatures but it can't use it to ward against Spirits (Preacher/Witch theme).
Choose one of the Sacred Verbs :
Create. Perceive. Transform. Destroy. Control. Protect
and a _Speed_
Quick or Slow
Quick Rituals are those done in a split second, spurs of the moment
Slow Rituals use clocks and are long time projects.
You choose your desired effect, the GM can help you find the correct Verb and Speed for the Ritual, and if fits with your Path's theme.
You gain 1d6 Stress each time you use a Ritual, you reduce this by your [[Basic System#Main Attributes|Grace]]
When rolling to see if the Ritual succeeds you roll as if it where a [[Basic System#Basic Roll|Basic Roll]] but using your Verb score instead of your attribute, some rituals might require using both your verbs and your attributes. If so you sum both and divide them by 2, rounding up
