### Beliefs
When a player creates a new character, they create some **Beliefs** for that character. These are not vague or general beliefs, such as "God" or "Country," but specific, actionable drives
They are a combination of an action and what surges from that action, a combination of a ***philosophy*** and a ***drive***
Examples of Beliefs include:
- "I must serve the King so that I might be redeemed for my crime."
- "I will protect my friend's sister at any cost."
- "I guard the prince's life with my own."
- "I hate Elves and I will not stop till each one is cleansed "

Acting in accordance with your belief grants you 1 **[[Banes and Boons|Boon]]** to that roll
Acting against your belief adds **1 [[Doom Dice]]** and gives you **1d6 Stress** 
>[!info]- **Change of Heart** 
Beliefs can evolve as the character grows and changes throughout the campaign. For instance, if a character holds the Belief, "I guard the prince's life with my own," and the prince dies, that Belief **must** change. Similarly, a Belief like "Orcs belong as slaves; giving them autonomy only brings ruin" might shift if the character spends time with autonomous Orcs and undergoes a change of perspective. However, since this is an internal process, the player may choose to retain the Belief if they feel the character’s mindset remains unchanged.
When a Belief is altered or discarded, it **must** be replaced with a new one. Ideally, the new Belief should tie meaningfully to the old one.
A Belief is so deeply rooted that changing it can inflict significant **Stress**. If the shift is gradual or born of positive revelation, it may come without cost. But if the change is sudden the new Belief may twist into something worse
###  Sins
Characters carry **Sins**, impulses that clash with what they believe to be good. This is not necessarily an objective or societal good, but rather what the character themselves perceives as wrong.
**Sins** are represented by a number that gets subtracted from  **20**. When faced with a situation related to their **Sin** that player's tempted to act upon it, if they fail, they act according to the **Sin** ,they gain **1d6 Stress**. Triggering a Sin (Failure or not) add **1 [[Doom Dice]]**
>[!Example]- List of Typical Sins
>  _Kleptomania, Blasphemy, Rudeness, Hermit, Battle Trauma_ or for more broad ones _Pride, Greed, Lust, Envy, Gluttony, Wrath, Sloth_

>[!Example]- Example in Play 
> if you got a Greed of **4** and you find a bag of golden coins when you're alone, you roll a **d20** against **16** ( 20 - **Greed**), if you roll higher than 16 you act according to your **Sin** and bag the gold for yourself. you take **1d6 stress** and add **1**  [[Doom Dice]]

###  Hope
Characters may gain **Hopes** strength they gained after facing adversities.
Hopes are chosen from a list, they grant  different benefits and are mechanical overrides, characters with enough [[Bond|Reputation]] are known for their Hopes
