**Bonds** are a combination of your own reputation,  your personal relationships or your relationship with factions. The values of **Bonds** range from -5 to 5. 
They each show how much **influence** and **fame** you have whit each  **Bond**, negative ones represent  **influence** and **fame** which brings trouble.
>[!Example]-
>A Criminal might have a **Bond of -3** with the **Local Guards**
>A Mage Student might have a **Bond of 3** with his **Wizard Teacher**

**Bonds** don't work just as a line between a Character and a Faction/NPC, if a Character has a good **Bond** with the local Priest, he may get a discount from a shopkeeper which is a regular visitor of the church, that same  **Bond** could bring some distrust from another church's clergy who know that Priest
All **Bonds** have two sides of a coin, a negative **Reputation** might be a good one for a Thieves Guild, a Good **Reputation** with a country might be horrible when visiting their neighbors at war 

>[!info]-
>Bonds don't have an explicit mechanical purpose, but NPCs also have **Bonds** written and they _should_ overlap with those of the Characters, when they do come up, they are often roleplayed or bring forth Boons, Banes or change the Difficulty of the Dice
